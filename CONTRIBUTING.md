# Contributing

Issues and discussion are welcome.

Pull requests that change normative text in SPEC.md require prior written agreement with Craig Thomas Ellrod as maintainer. Craig Thomas Ellrod is responsible for the editorial text of the specification.

Submitting a contribution does not create a patent grant from you, or from Craig Thomas Ellrod. Patent rights are reserved by Craig Thomas Ellrod. All contributors and implementers must follow the attribution requirements in NOTICE.md.

## Licensing of Contributions

- Contributions to specification text, schemas and test vectors are made under CC BY 4.0, as stated in LICENSE.md Part A4. A contribution grants no patent license except the commitment in Part A4(c).
- Every commit must carry a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (`git commit -s`, adding `Signed-off-by:`).
- By contributing, you grant for your contribution the same royalty-free patent commitment Craig Thomas Ellrod grants in LICENSE.md Part A2, on the same terms.
- Declare AI versus human contribution in AI_DISCLOSURE.md and ai_disclosure.json.
- Disclose any commercial interest in what you contribute, including any role with OpenAI, Stripe, a payment provider, or a merchant platform.
- Follow ETHICS.md.
